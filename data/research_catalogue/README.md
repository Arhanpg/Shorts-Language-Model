# Research catalogue releases

The research outputs are reconciled into a v0.1 candidate catalogue bundle. It contains discovery leads, taxonomy proposals and synthetic test fixtures—not videos or cleared training examples.

Install a downloaded release bundle with scripts/install_research_catalogue.py. The release is installed under data/research_catalogue/v0.1/; validate it with scripts/validate_research_catalogue.py.

Do not copy unverified creator leads into the active data/instagram_creator_seeds.txt or approved hashtag inputs. The release deliberately keeps unverified leads separate from verified operational seeds. All creator rights statuses are discovery-only; item-level rights approval remains mandatory before media processing or training.
