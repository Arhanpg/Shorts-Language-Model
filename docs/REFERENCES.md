## Licensing, consent and provenance

Open-source downloader availability does not establish a right to copy or train on a particular video. Check the media license, consent and all relevant third-party rights (including music). The application's rights manifest is an evidence pointer/permission record; it cannot authenticate an agreement. Obtain legal review for commercial collection/training and model releases.
