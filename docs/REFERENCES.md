## Citation and claim hygiene

Distinguish official documentation, peer-reviewed research, model cards, repository claims, third-party measurements and internal experiments. Record exact version/hash and date checked for each model/dataset. A public GitHub/Hugging Face page does not imply commercial-training or redistribution permission.
